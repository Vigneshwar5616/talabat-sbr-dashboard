All algorithms run in your browser on synthetic data generated from the SBR questionnaire: K-means, Apriori, logistic and linear regression, a CART decision tree, gradient boosting and XGBoost-style boosting (second-order gain, L2 regularisation, row and column sampling), two-model uplift, z-tests and t-tests. Survey figures are simulated, not talabat data; the P&L tab marks published figures as Reported and our estimates as Modelled.
